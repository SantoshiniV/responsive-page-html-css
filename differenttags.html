Computer Science

Computer science is the study of computers and computational systems, focusing on how they process information, solve problems, and interact with the world. Unlike computer engineering—which deals with physical hardware parts like chips and circuits—computer science is primarily focused on software, algorithms, and digital logic. It is the foundation behind everything from smartphone apps and search engines to artificial intelligence and cybersecurity
