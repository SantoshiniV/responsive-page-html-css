KERALA TRAVELS

Dream Destination Kerala

Kerala’s hill stations, backwaters, beaches, wildlife sanctuaries, and heritage towns make it one of India’s most diverse and scenic travel destinations.

Hill Stations

Kerala’s Western Ghats form one of the world’s richest biodiversity hotspots, and Munnar, set at about 1,600 m within these mountains, is a major hill station known for tea plantations, cool climate, wildlife, and scenic viewpoints.

Backwaters

The Kerala backwaters are a vast network of brackish lagoons, canals, lakes, and rivers running parallel to the Arabian Sea, spanning over 900 km

Beaches

Kerala offers a long 550‑km coastline with famous beaches like Varkala, Kovalam, Cherai, Marari, Bekal, and Muzhappilangad, each known for unique scenery and experiences.
